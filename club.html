Questions

Good to know

Can I skip or pause a shipment?

Yes. Skip any release with one click up to 7 days before it ships, or pause the club for as long as you like. No fees, no questions.

How much is shipping?

Free on every Cellar Circle shipment Australia-wide. Standard shipping applies to extra orders.

Do I need to be home to receive wine?

An adult must sign. Choose a work address, a neighbour or hold at the cellar door.

What if I do not like a wine?

Tell us within 30 days and we will replace it or credit your account.
