Our story

Old vines, unhurried hands

When Ruth and Tom Marlow bought a tired hillside in 1987, the oldest vines were already twenty-five years old. They kept them. Today their daughter Elise makes the wine the same way: low yields, wild ferments, no shortcuts.

Elise Marlow, winemaker
